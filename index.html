AI approach

AI helps with the first pass. I verify the results.

I used AI to help find and organise information in public reports and prepare an initial analysis. I then used Python and Excel to recalculate selected figures and check key inputs against their sources. I reviewed the results and considered which observations might need further investigation.

AI-assisted research

Gather and analyse

AI helped organise published information, review selected disclosures and suggest questions for analysis.

Independent checks

Recalculate and verify

I used Python and Excel to recalculate selected figures and trace key inputs back to published reports.

Human review

Interpret and decide

I considered the results in context and identified potential risks that might warrant further investigation.

Why use this workflow? AI can reduce time spent on information gathering and first-pass analysis, leaving more attention for verification and judgement. I have not measured time saved in this project. In one example, AI did not quantify an inventory-provision ratio movement; Python and Excel calculated it, and review identified a separate error in an intermediate human draft. The correction and its origin were documented. See the validation example.
