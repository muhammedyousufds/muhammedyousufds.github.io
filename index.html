Featured work

A public-source case study with clear limits.

The work connects disclosed financial information to questions an auditor might investigate, while keeping hypotheses separate from findings.

Project 01 · FY2021–FY2025 framing

Global Fashion Group S.A.

Assessed nine potential financial-statement risk categories, mapped relevant assertions and designed 45 proposed procedures. The underlying quantitative pilot covers FY2024–FY2025 only.

The independent baseline was frozen before a separate thematic comparison with EY’s published FY2025 Key Audit Matters (KAMs). This comparison does not evaluate audit quality.

What I found

  • Reported revenue fell 5.92% in euros; management reported a 0.6% decline at constant currency.
  • Gross margin increased while net inventory declined.
  • One €0.9m Pro-Forma Cash reconciliation difference remains unexplained.

How I work

Evidence first, conclusions kept proportionate.

01

Trace

Start with published disclosures and record the source, reporting perimeter and period.

02

Analyse

Separate reported amounts, calculated ratios and management-defined alternative performance measures.

03

Challenge

Describe a plausible risk hypothesis, the relevant assertion and evidence that would be needed to test it.

Evidence boundary: a separate public Python package now reproduces 15 selected calculations from sanitized public-source inputs. The original 76 Python–Excel cross-checks were completed internally; the full scripts, workbook and detailed log remain private and are not represented as publicly rerunnable.

Calculate and reconcile

Code and spreadsheet formulas calculated financial trends, ratios, GFG-defined NWC, the published Pro-Forma Cash bridge and sensitivity illustrations. A new public package lets visitors rerun 15 selected calculations.

Human review

Check and decide

I traced statements to annual-report pages, reperformed figures, separated fact from hypothesis and rejected unsupported causal claims. The €0.9m cash difference remains unexplained.

Example from the review record: the original AI draft did not quantify the inventory-provision ratio movement. A provisional human review draft gave 0.84 percentage points; recalculation showed 0.7995 points, rounded to 0.80. The 0.84 figure was a human drafting error, not an AI suggestion. Another broad supplier-finance inference was narrowed because the public disclosures do not map those balances to the NWC payable line. AI can omit context, misstate arithmetic or suggest explanations the evidence cannot support.
