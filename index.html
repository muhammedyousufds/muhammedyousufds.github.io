Featured project

What can public financial statements tell us about where audit attention may be needed?

An educational analysis of Global Fashion Group S.A. that connects published financial information to potential reporting risks and questions that could be investigated.

Public-source educational simulation

AI-Assisted Public Company Audit Analytics

What it does: examines financial trends and selected disclosures, identifies potential risk areas, maps them to relevant assertions, and proposes procedures that could be considered in an audit.

Why I built it: to practise linking accounting analysis to audit thinking, while showing where public information ends and further evidence would be needed.

This analysis uses published financial statements and disclosures. No underlying company ledgers, transaction records or other internal records were accessed; no proposed procedures were performed; and no audit opinion is expressed.

Project status

  • Completed pilot: FY2024–FY2025 quantitative analysis.
  • In progress: a consistent FY2021–FY2025 comparison.
  • Current output: nine potential risk categories and 45 proposed procedures.

AI approach

AI speeds up the first pass. I own the judgement.

I used AI to support research across selected public sources, organise relevant disclosures and prepare a first-pass analysis. I then used Python and Excel to independently recalculate and cross-check defined figures, traced key inputs to published reports, and applied my judgement to interpret results and challenge unsupported explanations.

AI-assisted research

Gather and analyse

AI helped locate and organise public information, review disclosures and prepare an initial analysis.

Independent checks

Recalculate and verify

Python and Excel independently recalculated defined metrics and reconciliations; I traced material inputs back to the published reports.

Human judgement

Interpret and decide

I assessed the context, challenged unsupported explanations and decided which potential risks warranted further investigation.

Why use this workflow? AI can reduce time spent on information gathering and first-pass analysis, leaving more attention for verification and judgement. I have not measured time saved in this project. In one example, AI did not quantify an inventory-provision ratio movement; Python and Excel calculated it, and review identified a separate error in an intermediate human draft. The correction and its origin were documented. See the validation example.
